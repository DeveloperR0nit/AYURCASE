Samsarjana Krama Dietary Schedule

After Virechana detoxification, the digestive fire (*Agni*) is tender like a tiny flame. Charaka Samhita prescribes a 3-day graded gruel diet to gradually rekindle metabolism without producing toxins (*Ama*).

Day 1: Peya (Clear Rice Water Gruel) 1 part red rice boiled with 14 parts water. Drink only the warm strained liquid seasoned with roasted cumin and pink rock salt. Quenches thirst and cleanses residual bile.
Day 2: Vilepi (Thick Rice Porridge) 1 part rice with 4 parts water. Includes soft grain substance. Easy to digest, provides physical strength, and restores intestinal lining.
Day 3: Krita Yusha (Spiced Moong Dal Soup) Thin yellow moong dal soup tempered with a drop of ghee, ginger slice, and fresh coriander. Fully restores hunger and normal appetite.
